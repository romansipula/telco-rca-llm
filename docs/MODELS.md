# Model choices and pre-training verification

Policy: no model of Chinese origin, and no fine-tune or distillation of one, anywhere in the
pipeline. That covers the base model, teacher, judge and embedding model. Excluded: Qwen, DeepSeek,
GLM, Yi, InternLM, MiniCPM, Baichuan, Kimi, Hunyuan, ERNIE.

Origin and licence come from the Hugging Face model cards and API tags (`license:apache-2.0`,
`base_model:` tags), checked on 2026-10-13. None of the three fine-tuned models lists a base or
distillation source outside its own vendor.

## Fit on a 16 GB T4 (fp16, no bf16), fp16 overflow, llama.cpp support

Measured by the verification kernel (`results/gpu_verify/`: 6 QLoRA steps on real prompts, fp16
finiteness probe, and a few zero-shot and SFT generations per model). The table is filled from
`results/gpu_verify/*/sft_evidence/train_log.json`; see the README for the final numbers.

llama.cpp support: `convert_hf_to_gguf.py` at llama.cpp commit `ace20f6` (2026-10-13) registers
`Gemma4ForConditionalGeneration`, `Mistral3ForConditionalGeneration` (`mistral3`), `GraniteForCausalLM`
and `Phi3ForCausalLM`. Each vendor also publishes official GGUFs (`google/gemma-4-E4B-it-qat-q4_0-gguf`,
`mistralai/Ministral-3-3B-Instruct-2512-GGUF`, `ibm-granite/granite-4.1-3b-GGUF`). The export of our
merged fine-tunes is the actual proof; see `results/export_*`.

## Why these settings

* **QLoRA:** NF4 base with double quantisation, fp16 compute, LoRA r=16, α=32 and dropout 0.05 on
  q/k/v/o/gate/up/down of the language model only (vision and audio towers excluded by regex).
  2 epochs, effective batch 16, LR 2e-4 with cosine decay and 5% warm-up. Loss is computed on the
  answer tokens only.
* **fp16 on T4:** Gemma models are known to overflow fp16 activations. Before training,
  `telco_rca.llm.train` runs a forward pass on three real examples and checks that loss and logits
  are finite. If they are not, it reloads with fp32 compute and records that in `train_log.json`
  (`fp32_fallback_used`).
* **GPU reference precision:** the fine-tuned models are evaluated with fp16 base weights plus the
  unmerged LoRA adapter (`infer_quant: none`). For Gemma 4 E4B (~16 GB in fp16 with its per-layer
  embeddings) this is spread over both T4s. This is the "GPU fp16" baseline that the GGUF quantisations
  are compared against.
